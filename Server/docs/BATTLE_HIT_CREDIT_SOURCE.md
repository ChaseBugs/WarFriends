# Recovered 1.4.0 PvP hit-credit provenance

This is the B24 rule for `StatsManager.MatchStats.hits`. It is narrower than damage, a projectile impact, or the Worker's `confirmed_enemy_hits`. No Backend reward or statistic should consume the latter as the source hit count.

| Client call path | What reaches `Weapon.ReportShotHit` | `MatchStats.hits` effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BulletBase.OnHit` | Calls `DoDamage(..., out damagedObject)` for the raycast collider, then passes that object to `ReportShotHit` even when damage is not applied. | One hit only if this is the local player's weapon, the shot is not a network copy, and `damagedObject` has a non-None fraction opposing the current player. A valid enemy shield contact can count even without HP loss. |
| `BulletPoison.OnHit` | Writes the target into `mHitDestroyableObject`, but passes a separate local `destroyableObject` initialized to null. | The recovered source does **not** increment this statistic on this path. Preserve this observable behavior until a deliberate Client change is authorized. |
| `GrenadeAmmoBase.Explode` and `MineAmmo` | Call `ReportShotHit` with a null destroyable object. | No `MatchStats.hits` increment from these calls, even if their explosions damage enemies. |

The shared predicate is in `Weapon.ReportShotHit`: `!isNetworkCopy`, `owner == PlayerController.currentPlayer`, PvP mode, and an opposing non-None `DestroyableObject.fraction`. The exact source sites are `Weapon.cs:341`, `BulletBase.cs:129-145`, `BulletPoison.cs:27-39`, `GrenadeAmmoBase.cs:205-213`, `MineAmmo.cs:165,179`, and `StatsManager.cs:1408` under `Clients/ExportedProject/Assets/Scripts/Assembly-CSharp`.

On the Worker, `MatchEngine` dispatches player bullets from `projectiles`, but also dispatches vehicle fire from `vehicleProjectiles`. Both can reach the same `ApplyArmyProjectileImpact`, `ApplyDecoyProjectileImpact`, and `ApplyHeavyTurretProjectileImpact` helpers. `confirmed_enemy_hits` is also incremented by land mines, army fire, turret fire, grenade/bazooka area damage, and direct player impacts. Its caller's player ID is not proof that the original source `Weapon.ReportShotHit` would have run.

The Worker now records `confirmed_player_bullet_hits` only while advancing its player-owned `projectiles` queue and emits it only in the private terminal-result snapshot used by the durable outbox. Gameplay UDP snapshots, including terminal replies, leave the field zero to preserve the 1,200-byte MTU. An enemy shield contact counts even without HP loss; a resolved opposing player or dynamic target counts at most once per bullet when its target handler confirms an enemy hit. The vehicle, army, turret, land-mine, fake-pellet, and grenade-area queues cannot increment this counter. Terminal validation requires it to be no higher than `confirmed_enemy_hits`, and `BattleResultStore.GetBulletHitEvidence` revalidates the durable result before exposing ordered per-player values. The collision result now retains the exact resolved collider layer from map, player, and dynamic targets, leaving it explicitly unknown in synthetic poses that supply no layer. That provenance is necessary for checking the Client's `TagsAndLayers.IsDestroyableObject` gate, but layer alone does not prove an exact-GameObject `DestroyableObject` component. This is a provenance-separated subset, not yet `MatchStats.hits`: player damage resolution may still differ from `BulletBase.DoDamage` on zero-damage contacts, source `BulletPoison` is excluded only because it is not in the admitted player-weapon catalog, and every playable weapon needs a running Unity comparison.

The direct bullet-credit paths now require a source-backed destroyable collision. A deployed Drone's layer-eight child sphere can still collide, but cannot credit the Client's `MatchStats.hits` because the recovered child GameObject lacks `DestroyableObject`; its layer-27 root can. Synthetic player poses without a source layer remain unproven and cannot credit this private statistic. The opposing-player path preserves credit when the Client-style damage callback refunds all health: `PlayerDamage.Resolve` still reports an applied contact and the private bullet counter rises. A dynamic target with no host-applied damage, such as a Drone during its immortal interval, remains a gap because dynamic credit currently follows `ConfirmedEnemyHits`. These checks do not yet make the entire private counter source-equivalent.

A deterministic live-Park host collision probe at the Drone's immortal tick shows why target identity cannot substitute for collider identity: a player muzzle ray toward the Drone root center resolves the layer-eight child sphere, while an exposed edge ray resolves the layer-27 root. Only the latter carries the same-GameObject destroyable provenance. The probe pins host geometry selection; it does not establish that a normal player Fire command produced the root contact or that the running Unity Client counts it. The immortal-root credit gap therefore remains open until a source-equivalent live impact can be reproduced and compared.

The Worker now passes its host-owned combat random source into every live rifle-family projectile preparation. A fixed critical-roll test proves the injected source reaches the recovered AK47 damage path; default production behavior still uses `Random.Shared.NextSingle`. This makes future shot preparation reproducible, but does not itself prove an immortal-root contact or Unity `MatchStats` parity.

Implementation gate: preserve exact impacted-collider destroyable/fraction identity, not only parent identity or HP change; add source ammo-class binding and the poison/null-target exceptions before admitting new weapons; compare each family against a running Unity Client; then promote only verified source-equivalent credits into an atomic statistics receipt. Existing broad host hit counters remain combat diagnostics, not economy or achievement authority.
