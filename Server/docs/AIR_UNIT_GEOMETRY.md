## Drone special authority checkpoint

`DroneSpecialState` now implements the recovered timer kernel: one initial float-range 5..10-second draw even without a purchased special, strict activation after that deadline, immunity lasting until the original deadline plus composed SPECIAL and a 0..1 draw, then a new deadline based on observed expiry time plus 10 minus SPECIAL and another 0..1 draw. One observation executes at most one source Update branch; it does not fast-forward missed cycles. Float random upper endpoints are accepted. Nonfinite/regressing clocks, invalid random samples, unsupported duration bounds, and deadlines that overflow or lose positive duration precision fail before publishing a phase change. The recovered Drone special lane begins at index 96 (1.5 seconds), ends at 116 (3.9), and has zero normal/elite contributions.

Expiry restores the existing lethal-removal lifecycle. Focused tests cover source boundaries, delayed observations, random call order, disabled/dead/non-running gates, corrupt inputs, immune registry damage, and an in-process match spanning activation/expiry ticks.

## Drone steering reconstruction

`DroneSteeringStep` reconstructs `DroneSteering.Steer` and the subsequent velocity/position integration, separately from the generic direct-to-destination `ArmyAirMotionState`. Source prefab values are mass 80, brake distance 0.6, brake speed 0.5, speed 0.87, corner delay 0.5, direction true and looping true. The source returns zero acceleration during a corner delay but still adds retained velocity to position; near a point it scales desired velocity by distance/brake distance, divides the velocity difference by mass, and adds velocity directly to position without another delta-time factor. Focused numerical cases cover these boundaries and vertical motion. Input bounds are explicit host validation policy, not recovered gameplay constants.

This kernel is not yet connected to normal deployment. Recovered path radius, per-point stay time, ordered waypoint identities, ten-percent direction reversal, looping/end rules, arrival callbacks, orientation and source-clock comparison must be bound and independently verified before replacing the stationary normal Drone row. The generic direct-motion primitive is not proof of recovered Drone flight fidelity.

`recovered-air-waypoint-routes.json`, generated and freshness-checked by `python Server/tools/extract_air_waypoint_routes.py --check`, now pins all 44 air spawn routes across the five maps (8 Aztec, 8 City, 10 Desert, 10 Park, 8 Snow).
