# Heavy Turret source boundary

The recovered `CardHeavyTurret` spawns exactly one `HeavyTurret`, passes `playerLevel / maxDisplayLevel` as its card progress, and lets `TurretBase.Spawn` choose the closest unused `TurretPosition` belonging to the owner's current defend position. It samples that transform against NavMesh area mask 1 within radius 10. The Client then interpolates the `CARDS_MIN` and `CARDS_MAX` rows through `VehicleBehaviourDefinititon.Interpolate`; integer batch bounds use the recovered truncating cast.

[`recovered-heavy-turret-source.json`](../content/recovered-heavy-turret-source.json) pins all **80** placements: two slots for each of eight defend positions across all five multiplayer maps. Every slot retains cover order, faction, main-cover flag, component/GameObject/transform identities, source position, and scene digest. It also pins the recovered `HeavyTurret.prefab` root, HeavyTurret and `TurretWeaponBasic` bindings, four mesh identities, three transformed collider boxes, batched-weapon reference, targeting flags, aim limits, prefab accuracy, and the two exact card rows. At zero-based player level 22, the source interpolation produces 858.6711 health, 55.07 damage, a truncated 3-6 batch, 2.5-5 second cooldown bounds, and a table accuracy of 0.775.

`HeavyTurretSourceCatalog` validates the complete artifact during Worker and Backend combat-package startup. It rejects missing slots, changed prefab identities, reordered maps/covers, duplicate component IDs, malformed vectors, and altered combat endpoints before allocation. `SelectNearestFree` reproduces the current-cover, owner-faction, closest-unused selection rule without accepting a Client position. Run `python Server/tools/extract_heavy_turret_source.py --check` after changing the recovered maps, prefab, source sheets, or extractor.

This slice establishes immutable placement and stat authority plus authenticated activation. `MatchEngine` now requires the selected `CardHeavyTurret`, reserves inventory, chooses the nearest free source slot on the player's current cover, samples it against the recovered NavMesh, composes the player's card-level health and weapon values, and publishes an idempotent bounded registry entry. Heavy Turrets are projected through protobuf snapshots and spawn/destruction events, owner cleanup releases their slots, and the portable client validates the typed state. Focused live-match tests cover placement, source stats, request replay, and exhausted inventory.

The host now owns the recovered initial/random cooldown, one-second aim, exclusive-upper-bound batch choice, 0.2-second cadence, and per-round real-shot selection. The table accuracy stays pinned as provenance, while the effective runtime probability is `1`: recovered `TurretBase.UpgradesLoaded` never copies the interpolated value into `TurretWeaponBasic.realShotProbability`, so its prefab value remains active. Target selection preserves the recovered Decoy-first, Rusher-first-unit, remaining-unit, then player fallback. The source visibility mask, 0.25-unit ray offset, 0.5-unit target clearance, muzzle transform, ArmyUpgrades speed of 25, player damage/overtime coefficient of 0.5, and `BulletSlow` 0.6-unit collision tail are pinned and run on the authoritative tick. Moving players use their recovered moving target and host route velocity for fly-time prediction. Bullets resolve static geometry, players, Decoys, infantry, vehicle bodies/passengers/repair drones, and opposing Heavy Turrets at impact time. Health mutation, damage/destruction events, slot release, and all three transformed source box colliders are authoritative, and snapshots expose attack state plus live Heavy Turret bullets.

The normal recovered card button now routes `CardHeavyTurret` through `UseHeavyTurretAsync` while a self-hosted match is connected and consumes the card only after `heavy-turret-spawned` or retry-safe `heavy-turret-replayed`. `SelfHostedHeavyTurretPresenter` reconciles authoritative snapshots with script-free copies of the recovered mesh/material hierarchy, retains visuals across health changes, removes them on destruction or snapshot disappearance, and plays the recovered metal/explosion presentation without applying local damage. The shared projectile presenter displays the recovered `BulletSlow` mesh for live Heavy Turret projectile rows and removes it on the host impact event. A Unity 2018.3 render audit proves all five recovered prefab mesh filters, authoritative placement, absence of gameplay scripts/colliders/rigidbodies, damage retention, event removal, and snapshot reconciliation.

The remaining player-target gap is the recovered aim-facing branch that chooses shield versus whole-body targets while hiding. Two-process/device proof is also required before Heavy Turret is playable end to end.
