# Ground vehicle source boundary

The recovered 1.4.0 deathmatch deployment catalog contains four ground-vehicle families: `ID_UNIT-HUMVEE` (`CarBehaviour`, speed 1.7), `ID_UNIT-TANK` (`TankBehaviour`, speed 1.0), `ID_UNIT-BUGGY` (`CarBuggyBehaviour`, speed 1.7), and `ID_UNIT-TRANSPORTER` (`CarTransporterBehaviour`, speed 1.7). Their `CanBeSpawned` methods require a same-faction free entry in `MapDefinition.spawnPointsCollectionCars`. `AICarBase.Spawn` chooses one of those free entries, reserves its `usedByEntity` field, places the vehicle at the spawn transform, and faces the first waypoint.

The version-2 recovered army spawn artifact now pins every ground-vehicle route directly from the five multiplayer scene files. It contains 20 `SpawnPointCar` components, four per map, and their 107 ordered `WaypointCircuit.waypointList.items` transforms. Each route also retains its component identity, final `SpawnPointCar.target`, `smoothRoute=false`, and `isLoop=false`. The extractor recomputes all world positions through the complete serialized transform chain, while `ArmySpawnPointCatalog` validates the artifact hash, exact schema, ordering, identities, finite coordinates, unique waypoint IDs, and final-target binding before publication.

Live ground-vehicle deployment now uses the generic army reservation as the authoritative `usedByEntity` equivalent, creates the matching generation-one vehicle-registry row and source attack state, and advances the ordered route at the unit's composed source speed on the 30 Hz host clock. The Army and vehicle snapshots publish the same position, arrival parks exactly at the final serialized target, and confirmed death removes motion, registry, and army authority together. A deterministic Park proof drives a Humvee and Tank from their separate reserved spawns to their final targets.

Vehicle `UpgradesLoaded` does not use the zeroed timing placeholders serialized on the behavior component. It replaces the primary turret with the selected upgrade rows. `ComposeVehicleShot` now reproduces that lane composition and binds the resulting immutable contract at spawn: stage-zero Humvee is probability 0.85, batch 4–5, cooldown 1.5–1.8 seconds; Tank is 0.8, 2–5, 2–5 seconds; Transporter is 0.9, 4–7, 3.5–4.5 seconds. All use the pinned speed-5 projectile. Trusted accuracy scales the probability and source-equivalent values over one become certainty. Buggy correctly retains a zero primary lane because its ordinary weapon is the separately configured cannon.

The four runtime prefabs now have a separate revision-pinned weapon graph. It validates seven turret roles and nine `BatchedWeapon` paths, including each turret component/type, aim time, rotation limit, visibility and target flags, prediction behavior, weapon component, cadence, projectile prefab identity, shot offset, and prefab-root-relative rest muzzle. The extractor removes the prefab-stage root translation because `Object.Instantiate(prefab, position, rotation)` replaces that pose; the catalog rotates the validated local muzzle with authoritative vehicle facing. Humvee's primary gun cadence is 0.3 seconds, Tank's is 0.1, and Buggy's primary placeholder is 0.5. Buggy's cannon owns two speed-2-cadence bazookas with a 0.25-second secondary delay. Transporter splits its batch over two 0.1-second `AutomaticRifle` paths. Cannon projectile identity remains distinct from the ordinary bullet prefab.

The recovered `AICarBase` uses Unity wheel physics, steering sensitivity, cautious angular speed, obstacle braking, and a two-unit final reach threshold. The host's current linear waypoint traversal preserves route order, speed ceiling, fixed-tick authority, and final position, but it has not yet been compared with recorded Unity vehicle trajectories and does not reproduce those transient wheel dynamics. Primary attack state now reproduces the source random initial cooldown, exclusive batch-size upper bound, per-round weapon cadence, real/fake roll, and random post-batch cooldown. Live route motion maintains a normalized host-facing vector, and the recovered strict turret rotation gate plus angle-proportional, 0.1-second-minimum aim duration are validated before scheduling. Automatic target selection, visibility proof, projectile launch, and collision still need to drive that scheduler. Cannon upgrade fields, passenger generation, multipart destruction, explosion damage, and normal Client vehicle presentation remain open B15 work.
