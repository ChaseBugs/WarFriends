# Ground vehicle source boundary

The recovered 1.4.0 deathmatch deployment catalog contains four ground-vehicle families: `ID_UNIT-HUMVEE` (`CarBehaviour`, speed 1.7), `ID_UNIT-TANK` (`TankBehaviour`, speed 1.0), `ID_UNIT-BUGGY` (`CarBuggyBehaviour`, speed 1.7), and `ID_UNIT-TRANSPORTER` (`CarTransporterBehaviour`, speed 1.7). Their `CanBeSpawned` methods require a same-faction free entry in `MapDefinition.spawnPointsCollectionCars`. `AICarBase.Spawn` chooses one of those free entries, reserves its `usedByEntity` field, places the vehicle at the spawn transform, and faces the first waypoint.

The version-2 recovered army spawn artifact now pins every ground-vehicle route directly from the five multiplayer scene files. It contains 20 `SpawnPointCar` components, four per map, and their 107 ordered `WaypointCircuit.waypointList.items` transforms. Each route also retains its component identity, final `SpawnPointCar.target`, `smoothRoute=false`, and `isLoop=false`. The extractor recomputes all world positions through the complete serialized transform chain, while `ArmySpawnPointCatalog` validates the artifact hash, exact schema, ordering, identities, finite coordinates, unique waypoint IDs, and final-target binding before publication.

This closes source discovery and immutable validation for the scene routes. The current live deployment path still creates ground units through the generic army entity flow and does not yet reserve a vehicle spawn, advance the recovered route with the Client's wheel/steering behavior, park at the final transform, create passengers, or bind the existing vehicle attack registry to that deployed entity. Those are the next B15 implementation steps; the pinned route data supplies their required scene authority without inventing paths.
