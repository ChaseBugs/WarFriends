Their `CanBeSpawned` methods require a same-faction free entry in `MapDefinition.spawnPointsCollectionCars`. `AICarBase.Spawn` chooses one of those free entries, reserves its `usedByEntity` field, places the vehicle at the spawn transform, and faces the first waypoint.
