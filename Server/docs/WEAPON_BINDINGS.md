# Complete recovered weapon binding graph

`content/recovered-all-weapon-bindings.json` is a Unity 2018 export from the recovered 1.4.0 `MainScene`. It binds every one of the 66 `Google2u.WeaponUpgrades` definitions to its unique `WeaponInventory` index, `WeaponLevelsSetup`, `PlayerWeapon` input controller, concrete `Weapon`, animation-family enum, muzzle or throw origin, ammo-setup component, and projectile prefab GUID/file ID/SHA-256. The artifact is generated by `SelfHostedAllWeaponExport.Run`; it opens the scene read-only and does not run account initialization or save assets.

`WeaponBindingGraphCatalog` validates the artifact during combat-package startup. Validation requires the exact field schema and MainScene digest; the exact 66 source IDs from `recovered-battle-content.json`; unique inventory indexes `0..66` except the source gap at 20; complete component relationships; bounded identity/path fields; and projectile GUID, file ID, and digest shapes. It also requires the recovered controller distribution (27 click, 16 burst, eight zoom, seven hold, seven swipe, one minigun) and concrete weapon distribution. Duplicate indexes, missing/replaced definitions, changed component graphs, and package digest drift fail before a match is admitted.

This graph completes identity discovery, not combat behavior. The eleven assault-rifle families have source-validated stat, animation, muzzle, projectile, and live-UDP behavior. Their equipped slots are now bound to the exact graph index; authoritative switching retains per-slot ammunition, reload and cadence state, and the live Unity audit switches AK-47 index 11 to FAMAS index 26. [All eight SMGs and 398 upgrade stages](SMG_SOURCE.md) now have a separate validated stat/controller/muzzle/projectile package; their three-round scheduler, live UDP mode, mixed-slot switching and Unity presentation are implemented and remain unscored. Shotguns have a separate validated package and live mode; mixed host switching reaches their source volley path, while normal Unity shotgun selection remains open. All six pistols and 306 stages now have a validated package, live click/projectile/reload behavior, source infinite reserve, mixed-slot switching and Unity presentation. The eight non-minigun LMG families and 398 stages have validated two-round burst authority and Unity presentation. Sniper, launcher, grenade, and the dedicated minigun class-specific cadence/gesture/projectile/effect simulations remain closed until their recovered behaviors are implemented and compared in Unity.
