# WarFriends repository guidance

## Recovered-client sources

- Treat `Client` as the 4.9.5 resource source of truth.
- Treat `Client-Decompiled-Mono-1.6.0` as the recovered C# script, protocol, and behavioral-contract source.
- When the two versions differ, document the evidence and keep any server fallback explicit; do not invent retired live-ops prices, schedules, weights, or rewards.

## Server implementation rules

- Implement `Server` behavior from recovered client contracts and authoritative server state. Client-provided prices, rewards, scores, ownership, and progression are assertions, never authority.
- Keep every currency, item, reward, receipt, and claim transition atomic and retry-safe. Validate a nonnegative server-authored currency reward with `checkedRewardBalance` before publishing its claim or receipt marker.
- Apply that guard to direct, one-time, and tutorial rewards; conversion proceeds; level-up bonuses; PvP settlement components; and duplicate-item compensation. Do not reintroduce one-off `balance + reward` checks.
- Validate server-owned assignment, starter-assignment proof, achievement, War Arena, and Squad War eligibility/placement counters and receipt timestamps as nonnegative safe integers before comparisons, increments, or claim markers; `NaN` and `Infinity` must never satisfy a reward gate or placement through JavaScript comparison behavior.
- Validate the complete durable `AchievementsData` container at shared progression read/publication and before normalization, acknowledgement, increment, claim, or serialization. Require exact outer/group/tier shapes, bounded unique nonnegative group IDs, signed-client-safe offsets and nonnegative values, bounded known/unknown tier arrays, sequential Boolean claims, known values no higher than their final recovered target, and no claimed known tier whose target is unearned. Missing groups and short known tier arrays remain explicit schema-migration inputs; well-formed unknown groups may be preserved but never map to a reward without a server definition.
- Validate the complete War Arena lifecycle at shared progression read/publication and before boot, entry, battle start/settlement, heart/life changes, rewards, or serialization. Require known fields, safe counters and bounded timestamps, Boolean flags, bounded unique opponents and shown-event IDs, a bounded unique terminal-settlement history with supported end reasons and bounded JSON responses, and at most one active receipt bound to the same Arena and no terminal receipt. Only the absent legacy `shownArenaIds` list may normalize empty; malformed history must never be truncated into replay capacity or make a reward gate appear satisfied.
- Validate the complete Daily Missions lifecycle at shared progression read/publication and before rollover, boot, start/settlement, rewards, or serialization. Require the exact three daily and five ordered heroic definitions/config/unit shapes, consistent completion/reward flags and heroic prefix, bounded levels/reward cursor/heroic points, one derived UTC reset/day key, a source-backed or explicitly empty legacy heroic-unit target, and bounded unique active/terminal receipts with valid modes/indexes/end reasons/timestamps and bounded JSON responses. Expired cycles must still validate before rollover because heroic progress and replay receipts carry forward; malformed history must never be truncated into replay capacity.
- Validate the complete limited-time Event Assignment snapshot at shared progression read/publication and before boot, initialization, trusted progress, claims, or serialization. Require an exact safe event ID, lowercase SHA-256 config digest, nonnegative signed-client total, canonical bounded sparse day indexes with exact `{v,c}` records, and canonical bounded sparse milestone indexes whose present values are only `true`. Against the active immutable config, cap each day at its target, require a claimed day to reach its target, derive `totalValue` exactly from claimed-day point rows, and require milestone claims to form an earned prefix; malformed or config-inconsistent progress must never be reset into a fresh claimable event.
- Validate Player League competition mirrors (`MedalsBalance` and `Skill`), beginner tier, normal tier, and the exact zero-or-one placement counter before boot, tutorial repair, admission, ranking, or settlement; one corrupt member must abort a reward settlement before its first write.
- Validate the complete `StarterAssignmentsData` snapshot before account creation, boot projection, completion, or claim. Its deadline must be a bounded safe Unix second, only the ten recovered IDs may exist, record flags must be Boolean, and a claimed record must also be completed; corrupt deadlines must never make onboarding rewards permanently claimable.
- When a buffered starter action rejects corrupt durable onboarding state, return an expired empty `StarterAssignmentsData` error view without mutating or reserializing the damaged snapshot; one invalid action must not escape its recovered per-action RequestBuffer result.
- Validate the complete daily-assignment snapshot at shared progression read/publication and before rollover, gameplay mutation, or serialization. Require the exact three server-supported records in recovered order with source IDs/targets, finite fractions, consistent done/claimed flags, a completed counter equal to claimed records, a Boolean skip marker, and a nonnegative safe mega cursor. The `issued`/`tomorrow`/`dayKey` tuple must describe one derived UTC cycle with bounded safe Unix seconds; a non-finite reset must never freeze one assignment day permanently.
- Validate a current-month daily-reward calendar as one exact ordered authority snapshot at shared progression read/publication and before check, claim, or serialization: year/month and `lastCheckDay` must describe a real UTC date, and `0 <= claimReward <= canClaim <= daysInMonth`. Capture one server time for both sides of a publication. Discard an expired prior-month snapshot before validating its cursors because it carries no claim authority forward, but reject malformed calendar coordinates or a future-month snapshot rather than treating it as expired.
- Validate the complete rewarded-video state at shared progression read/publication and before grant or boot projection. Every one of the four exact ledgers must contain only positive safe Unix seconds within its source cap. The exact private replay receipt must bind a supported reward, safe timestamp, non-future revision, matching ledger grant, and bounded JSON response containing that same ledger snapshot. Only valid entries provably outside their rolling window may be discarded; malformed entries or receipts must never be treated as expired or trusted for replay because that reopens reward capacity or returns forged results.
- Validate the complete ranked-PvP win-streak pair at shared progression read/publication and before settlement or boot projection: `WinCount` must be exactly 0-9, the zero count must pair only with timestamp zero, and a positive count needs a signed-client-`int` Unix second. Keep shared transaction publication deterministic by applying the non-future comparison only against the authoritative boot/settlement request time. Never clamp corruption into the maximum WarBucks tier or expose a future timestamp through the client's `isInfinite` path.
- Validate the paid-VIP lootbox countdown as an exact 1-4 integer before settlement, progression migration, boot projection, or shared progression publication; only an absent legacy field defaults to four. Before duplicate conversion, reject a selected visual whose persisted parts exceed its catalog target instead of clamping unproven ownership into WarBucks.
- Validate the complete Instant Battle public counter tuple and private replay receipt at shared progression read/publication and before readiness, settlement, or boot. Timer, lifetime battles, and paid batches must be nonnegative signed-client integers; paid five-battle groups cannot exceed the lifetime count. The receipt must retain its exact fields, source price/count bounds, level/refill consistency, and a non-future revision. Keep shared publication deterministic by applying timer/receipt non-future time checks only at authoritative boot/action request time. Do not clamp corrupt authority into a valid recharge, replay, reward, or Gold-price state.
- Validate the persisted squad-creation count at shared progression read/publication and before price calculation, mutation, or boot so `(count + 1) * 25` remains positive and representable by the recovered client's signed `int`. Validate the WarBucks wallet, progression revision, and next count before debit; malformed arithmetic must never publish a squad or counter or survive an unrelated progression replacement.
- Validate profile-owned `renameCount` before price calculation, error recovery, mutation, or PlayerAnalytics boot. Counts 0-29 may be displayed, but a mutation must validate the increment before debit so count 29 cannot publish the signed-overflowing count 30; validate Gold and progression revision before the atomic profile/count update.
- Validate the complete `collectedRewards` dictionary before progression migration, one-time grant/replay, PlayerAnalytics boot, or shared progression publication. Preserve bounded legacy IDs, but require every present marker to be exact integer `1` because the client uses key presence; any other value must not let the backend pay a reward the client already considers collected.
- Validate the squad-chat read cursor at shared progression read/publication and before action-193 comparison, revision mutation, or PlayerAnalytics boot, using one captured server time for both sides of publication. Only an absent legacy value defaults to zero; the cursor must be a nonnegative signed-client integer within the bounded server-time skew window, remain monotonic across devices, and never let `NaN`, `Infinity`, or a far-future value hide later messages or survive an unrelated replacement.
- Validate the complete feature-introduction snapshot at shared progression read/publication and before any one-way shown action or PlayerAnalytics boot. Only the seven recovered keys may exist and every present value must be Boolean; missing legacy fields default to false, malformed truthy values must never suppress onboarding UI or survive an unrelated progression replacement, and a real false-to-true transition must validate progression revision before mutation.
- Validate the complete ordered tutorial lifecycle at shared progression read/publication and before bootcamp start/finish, Play Warcards start/settlement, rental eligibility, or boot projection. Only absent legacy completion markers default to false; both stored values must be Boolean and Play Warcards completion requires bootcamp completion. Each optional battle receipt must contain exactly a bounded control-free ID and a safe Unix start time, terminal phases must not retain consumed receipts, and every real transition must validate progression revision before publishing its receipt or marker. Keep shared transaction publication deterministic by applying the non-future comparison only against authoritative boot/gameplay request time.
- Bind each real-money purchase action to its reviewed entitlement family; sharing store-proof verification must never let a token cross into an incompatible response or grant path.
- Validate the complete paid-VIP and exact four-field subscription timelines as nonnegative safe-integer Unix seconds at their shared read/publication boundaries. Paid VIP keeps expiry as the recovered active-benefit predicate, but its display start must be zero with no entitlement and otherwise not exceed expiry. Subscription authority must remain one ordered interval (`subscribeSince <= dogTagTimerLock <= expireTime`); activity requires `subscribeSince <= now < expireTime`, and boot must reject a future start before serializing the expiry-driven stock-client model. A non-finite entitlement expiry must never become permanent access through JavaScript comparison behavior or an invalid durable receipt date.
- Validate every present subscription authority receipt ID as the exact 64-character lowercase hexadecimal HMAC-SHA256 key generated by purchase delivery before read, comparison, or publication. Absence remains the explicit legacy state; arbitrary readable IDs are test-only inputs and never durable authority.
- Validate the complete RequestBuffer replay cache and message-ignore outbox before lookup, acknowledgement, read, or publication. Replay entries must be bounded, uniquely identified by trimmed control-free BufferIds, contain safe Unix timestamps and parseable result arrays, and outbox IDs must be bounded, unique, trimmed, and control-free; malformed rows must fail closed rather than be discarded and reopen buffered economy actions.
- Validate the complete action-221 WarBucks conversion receipt before replay lookup, read, or publication. Accept only the twelve recovered A/B IDs with their exact source Gold debit, a positive safe grant, safe time, and a non-future progression revision; malformed receipts must fail closed rather than be overwritten and reopen a recent conversion.
- Validate the complete paid-VIP daily-card marker before grant comparison, read, or publication. Its UTC day must derive exactly from a safe non-future grant timestamp and its two reward IDs must be implemented recovered Silver/Gold cards, with duplicates allowed; malformed markers must never be overwritten to reopen the daily pair.
- Validate the private War Card play and Gold-card craft lifetime counters as nonnegative safe integers at shared read/publication and reward-derivation boundaries. Missing legacy counters normalize to zero; malformed values must never satisfy starter assignments, achievements, or survive an unrelated progression replacement.
- Validate the complete `CraftData` receipt before boot projection, crafting actions, failure serialization, or progression publication. Only the exact empty zero tuple or three implemented same-rarity Bronze/Silver IDs with the exact source duration is valid; both timestamps must fit the recovered signed C# `int`, and malformed partial state must fail closed rather than reopen already-consumed inputs.
- Validate the complete five-field `CardManagerData` snapshot before boot projection, card gameplay, failure serialization, or progression publication. Normal ownership must remain sparse positive signed-client counts of implemented IDs; Buddy ownership must remain within the source cap with exact bounded loadout records and source-backed unit/weapon indexes; cooldowns must fit the recovered signed C# `int`; and `extraSlot` must be Boolean. Only wholly missing legacy inventory may materialize empty defaults.
- Validate the complete `InventoryData`/`LevelManagerData` snapshot at shared progression read/publication and before mutation or serialization. Require the exact four recovered weapon slots with source-backed identity/index/category/ownership, exact saved weapon/unit record shapes with bounded catalog cursors and consistent ownership flags, the recovered two-per-deployment-category and three-mechanical equipped-unit caps, and either the exact empty delivery tuple or one active delivery matching a permanent saved upgrade cursor. Only a wholly absent legacy item inventory may materialize the recovered starter loadout; malformed nested authority must fail closed rather than be defaulted or survive an unrelated replacement.
- Validate the complete rental lifecycle at shared progression read/publication and before boot projection, loadout, Army Power, acceptance, settlement, or permanent redemption. Only source-backed weapon/unit types, 20-25% discounts, safe timestamps, and status-consistent trial/sale intervals may authorize borrowed inventory; corrupt deadlines must fail closed rather than become permanent access or survive an unrelated replacement.
- Validate the complete Black Market set at shared progression read/publication and before boot projection, action-217 replay/rotation, serialization, or buffered weapon redemption. Expiry and issue cursors must be safe integers; offer identities must be unique and bounded; feature authority remains zero until the retired weighting source is recovered.
- Validate the complete visual inventory at shared progression read/publication and before boot projection, purchase, ownership/equip checks, paid-pack extension, or voided-purchase reversal. Timed `expiresOn` values and part counters must be bounded safe integers, saved flags and equipped slots must retain the recovered shape, and non-finite expiry must never turn a temporary cosmetic into permanent access or survive an unrelated replacement.
- Keep account sanctions as audited server-owned records. Enforce active bans through the shared authentication path only after credential proof, evaluate temporary expiry from application time rather than TTL cleanup, return the recovered `AccountBanned` fields, and require retry-safe operator issue/revoke keys.
- Keep moderation report review transitions optimistic and append-audited: require the operator's expected status, never reopen a terminal report, and replay only an identical actor/decision/note operation.
- Keep player appeals ownership-scoped and separate from stock gameplay actions. Accept one appeal per active sanction through the current session credential, expose no private sanction fields, require optimistic append-audited review/withdrawal transitions, and revoke an accepted appeal's live sanction in the same transaction.
- Keep moderation retention explicit and operator-driven: freeze one preview timestamp for bounded export and purge, delete only terminal reports/appeals older than configured cutoffs, retain sanctions indefinitely, and publish the purge counts in the same idempotent transaction as deletion.
- Rotate `AUTH_SECRET` through the bounded fallback-key overlap documented in `Server/README.md`: keep gameplay sessions valid, verify durable password/provider digests against fallbacks only, and compare-and-set rehash a successful fallback login with the active key before retiring the old key.
- Treat each stored custom-password scrypt factor as verification authority within the bounded supported range. Raising `AUTH_SCRYPT_COST` may upgrade a successful login, but a temporary lower setting must never downgrade an already stronger digest.
- Keep ordinary offline-bot battles outside ranked settlement. Their action-64 metadata may select a durable zero-reward lifecycle only after server-owned Play Warcards eligibility is checked; client-simulated bot results must never grant or consume economy, progression, cards, rentals, achievements, assignments, leagues, or events.
- Keep production MongoDB backups authenticated and encrypted before they leave temporary local storage. Retention may delete only an old, path-confined archive/manifest pair whose database, size, and SHA-256 all validate; malformed, damaged, unrelated, or orphaned files must remain for operator review.
- Keep monitoring artifacts on the bounded metrics contract actually exported by `metricsService`. Alerts must require sustained signals and meaningful traffic where ratios are used; Redis alerts apply only to scrape targets explicitly labeled `redis_required=true`.
- Preserve legitimate negative Gold or WarBucks chargeback debt, but reject non-safe-integer rewards and arithmetic overflow.
- Keep the shared DynamoDB numeric wire adapter fail-closed across boot, public profile, experience leaderboard, and Arena leaderboard responses. It may preserve the recovered integer projection for legitimate finite fractional values such as calculated Army Power, but a non-finite value or unsafe projected integer must abort the response instead of being serialized as a believable zero. Do not introduce local numeric helpers that bypass this boundary.
- Validate core progression balances at the shared persisted read boundary, before `PlayerData` boot projection, and again on every progression mutation result before publication. Gold and WarBucks may be safe-integer chargeback debt; Tickets, Scraps, and level experience must be nonnegative safe integers, and malformed values must never bypass a price comparison or be silently serialized as zero.
- Validate the complete dog-tag tuple shape at every shared progression read/publication and before boot projection, regeneration, counting, spend, or refill. Callers that own an authoritative request/boot time must supply one captured server time for both snapshots and enforce a non-future cursor; context-free publishers must remain deterministic while still rejecting every malformed tuple value. Refill seconds and the divisible base cap must be positive safe integers; base seconds must remain between the normal cap and the exact two-tag VIP debt floor; malformed time must never freeze regeneration through min/max behavior or survive an unrelated replacement. Detect the legacy count-only schema by canonical-field absence, never by numeric validity, so corrupt canonical values cannot be silently reconstructed.
- Validate progression revision and core-balance authority through the shared publication validator for ordinary writes and every custom full-document replacement, including external-entitlement transactions. Only an absent legacy revision may migrate to zero; every produced revision must advance monotonically as a nonnegative safe integer, and both snapshots must retain valid core balances before MongoDB can publish them. RequestBuffer may advance multiple internal steps in one atomic replacement.
- Keep all custom progression publishers on that shared successor proof: inbox, Google Play delivery/revalidation/reversal, missions, Instant Battle, rename, Squad creation/cards/departure, tutorial, Squad War, PvP, and operator integrity repair. A tutorial profile-only repair must omit the unchanged progression field; an operator repair must reload and validate the exact audited `updatedAt` snapshot before replacement.
- Require the exact supported embedded progression `schemaVersion` at the shared read and publication boundaries. A wholly absent progression may use the new-account fallback, but a missing, malformed, or future version inside a present document must fail closed until an explicit migration understands it.
- Keep reward-bearing inbox claims in the same MongoDB transaction as their terminal message marker. Validate their pure wallet/revision successor and bind the player replacement to the stored progression revision so a stale custom transaction cannot overwrite unrelated economy state.
- Fail closed when authoritative combat evidence, live-event configuration, or source-backed balancing is unavailable.
- Add detailed English comments where recovered names are opaque or atomicity, replay, migration, or fallback decisions are not obvious.

## Change discipline

- Run `npm test` from `Server` for backend changes.
- Update `Server/BACKEND_FEATURES.md` and `Server/README.md` when backend behavior or a documented gap changes.
- Keep unresolved backend gaps explicit. Do not mark a feature complete while its authoritative source or external provider integration is missing.
- The worktree may contain unrelated recovered-client changes. Stage and commit only files belonging to the current increment.
